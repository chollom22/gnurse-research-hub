Research Information

Research Title:
Assessment of the Diphtheria Outbreak in Plateau State, Nigeria: Knowledge, Risk Factors, Vaccination Status, Health Effects and Prevention Practices

This questionnaire is being used to collect information about people's knowledge, awareness, vaccination practices, possible risk factors and perceptions concerning the reported diphtheria outbreak in Plateau State.

Privacy: This questionnaire does not ask for your name, phone number, password or other direct personal identifiers. Your responses will be used for research and statistical analysis.
Important: This questionnaire does not diagnose diphtheria. If you have symptoms or have been exposed to a suspected case, seek medical attention from an appropriate healthcare facility.
