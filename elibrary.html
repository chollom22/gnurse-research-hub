🌍 How Related Libraries Work

The system can connect to legitimate library catalogues that provide public APIs, feeds or approved integrations.

This does not mean G Nurse copies or hosts every external book. Where a source only provides catalogue information, G Nurse displays the catalogue result and directs the reader to the approved source.

Where a legitimate source provides an authorized reading option, the reader can use that option.

External services may have their own terms, copyright rules, API limits and commercial restrictions.

💰 Earning From Related Libraries

G Nurse can potentially earn from approved affiliate or referral programmes when an external library or publisher offers one.

G Nurse may also place approved advertisements around its catalogue/search experience.

Simply sending someone to another library does not automatically create a payment. The external service must provide an approved referral, affiliate or advertising arrangement.
